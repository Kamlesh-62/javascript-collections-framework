Add `LinkedHashMap`, a map in insertion order that can be read and changed at both ends, with the `SequencedMap` and `ReadonlySequencedMap` types and the `LinkedHashMapOptions` type. It behaves as `HashMap` does — native `Map` names and results, SameValueZero by default, the `key` and `equivalence` options, live iteration and live entries — and adds `firstEntry`, `lastEntry`, `pollFirstEntry` and `pollLastEntry`, `putFirst` and `putLast`, which insert an absent key at that end or move the present mapping there, and a live `reversed()` view whose inserts go to the view's end; all are O(1). With `accessOrder: true`, reading or writing a present key moves its mapping to the end; with `maxSize`, inserting past the bound evicts from the front, never the mapping just inserted, and `onEvict(value, key, map)` is told about each eviction after the call, delivered in a loop so re-entrant calls never grow the stack — together an LRU cache. An access move never makes an open loop return a mapping twice. Its `keySet()`, `entrySet()` and `valueCollection()` views are sequenced, with the map's ends and reversed views.

`SortedMap` and `ReadonlySortedMap` now extend `SequencedMap` and `ReadonlySequencedMap`, and their `entrySet()` and `valueCollection()` are typed `SequencedSet` and `SequencedCollection` (type-only narrowing). `TreeMap` and its range and reversed views gain `putFirst` and `putLast`, which always throw `UnsupportedOperationError` without checking or changing anything, and carry the sequenced-map brand. `TreeMap`'s entry and value views gain `getFirst`, `getLast`, `removeFirst`, `removeLast` (throwing `NoSuchElementError` when empty), `reversed()` and throwing `addFirst`/`addLast`, and `isSequencedCollection` now answers `true` for them.

Map equality now asks the other map through `entrySet().has(entry)`, so comparing never reorders an access-ordered map. For `HashMap` and `TreeMap` the results are unchanged, with two side effects: values are compared as the other map's value against this map's (`equals(otherValue, thisValue)`), and a value whose `equals` adds or removes mappings of the other map now makes the comparison throw `ConcurrentModificationError`.
